# Brickwood Valley — walkthrough

A 12-mission campaign for WorldBuilder 2's Jungle world. Each mission adds
one new idea and the finale combines them. Every mission has been checked
with `tools/simulate.js` against a full solution (in
`campaign/design/levels.js`); rebuild and re-check them all with:

```
node campaign/design/build.js
```

Each mission below has a **hint** and then the **solution** (spoilers!).

---

### 1. First Bricks — *build your first model*
**Hint:** drive onto plans to collect them; build next to brick piles.
**Solution:** drive the buggy onto the duck plan, build a duck next to the
yellow bricks and swim it to the goal in the pond. **Bonus:** pick up the
snail plan with the buggy, take the buggy apart and build a snail from its
red bricks.

### 2. Rocky Road — *rocky ground and recycling models*
**Hint:** only some models cross rocky ground; your buggy has the wheels you need.
**Solution:** collect the dirtbuggy plan, take the buggy apart next to the
yellow bricks and build a dirtbuggy. Cross the rocks to the goal.
**Bonus:** grab the duck plan, take the dirtbuggy apart by the pond and
build a duck.

### 3. Heavy Lifting — *carrying bricks*
**Hint:** a dumptruck carries 25 bricks; the gas station refills it.
**Solution:** collect the house plan, then haul the green, blue, yellow and
white piles one at a time to the tiles around the goal (recharge at the gas
station between trips) and build the house on the goal.
**Bonus:** collect the forklift plan, take the dumptruck apart next to the
red bricks and build a forklift.

### 4. Bridge Builder — *the steamshovel*
**Hint:** a steamshovel can only fill water with earth it dug up.
**Solution:** collect the steamshovel plan, recycle the buggy's wheels and
energy into a steamshovel, then cross the river: dig the tile behind you,
fill the water in front, step forward, repeat. Drive to the goal.
**Bonus:** collect the buggy plan, take the steamshovel apart next to the
red bricks and build a buggy.

### 5. Crab Cove — *defenders and monster bricks*
**Hint:** a defended crab drops its bricks — you need red ones.
**Solution:** collect the defender plan and build a defender. Take the crab
in the den apart, grab the snail plan and build a snail from the crab's
red bricks. Walk it to the goal.
**Bonus:** the second crab guards the bonus goal on the right — defeat it
and walk the defender in.

### 6. Treebot Trail — *moving trees*
**Hint:** a treebot holds one tree at a time; plant it before pulling the next.
**Solution:** build the treebot and tunnel along the middle row of the
forest: uproot a tree, plant it in one of the small clearings beside you,
step forward, repeat. Then drive the buggy to the goal.
**Bonus:** open the tree ring around the bonus goal and walk the treebot in.

### 7. Boulder Pass — *pushing boulders*
**Hint:** a dumptruck holds exactly the bricks a bulldozer needs.
**Solution:** collect the dozer plan, take the dumptruck apart and build a
bulldozer. Push the boulder through the canyon into the open area, then
push the second boulder aside to open the summit and drive onto the goal.
**Bonus:** collect the duck plan near the summit and build a duck from the
leftover yellow bricks and the spare energy brick.

### 8. Island Hopping — *water models and ferrying*
**Hint:** only fish and boats cross deep (dark) water; a tugboat carries 5 bricks.
**Solution:** with the duck, collect the fish and snail plans. Build a fish
and fetch the tugboat plan from the deep channel. Build the tugboat, ferry
the 5 red bricks to the east island and build a snail there.
**Bonus:** take the duck apart on the west shore, ferry its 5 yellow bricks
across and rebuild it with the energy brick on the east island.

### 9. Running on Empty — *energy management*
**Hint:** you can build a gas station of your own.
**Solution:** build a gas station in the little alcove near the end of the
road (the bricks are there). Haul the white bricks, then the red and
yellow bricks to the goal, recharging at the stations, and build the garage
on the goal (its energy brick is already there).
**Bonus:** take the dumptruck apart next to the red bricks by the goal and
build a buggy.

### 10. Frozen Fury — *the freezebot*
**Hint:** a frozen monster can't hurt you — and a bulldozer can push it.
**Solution:** build a freezebot and walk it within two tiles of the T-Rex
to freeze it, then race the buggy past to the goal. Stay close so it keeps
it frozen.
**Bonus:** build a bulldozer and push the frozen T-Rex down into the pen.

### 11. Scorpion Stronghold — *guard towers*
**Hint:** defenders lose to scorpions; guard towers don't.
**Solution:** build a guard tower beside the gate; it defeats the scorpion
inside. Bring yellow bricks into the fortress and build a second tower from
the first scorpion's red bricks to defeat the other. Drive the dumptruck to
the goal.
**Bonus:** carry the second scorpion's red bricks to the far corner and build
a tower there with the supplies waiting for you.

### 12. The Last Expedition — *everything together*
**Hint:** this time the bridge has to stay — dig your earth from beside the path.
**Solution:** build a treebot and tunnel through the forest. Collect the
defender and steamshovel plans; recycle the dirtbuggy into a steamshovel and
build a defender. Build a lasting bridge (dig from the bank next to the
bridge, never behind you), send the defender over to defeat the crab,
collect the house plan and build the house on the goal.
**Bonus:** collect the snail plan and build a snail from the crab's bricks.
